# Limitations

- Records are collector-observed unless provider-origin authentication exists.
- Independent reruns are corroboration but do not establish statistical independence.
- Missing runs remain missing; never manufacture earlier observations.
- Probe design measures evidential reasoning on synthetic scenarios; not legal determinations.
- Collection coverage depends on authorized model access. As of 2026-11-08 the public tree has the harness and zero authentic records. See `COVERAGE.md`.
- OpenAI is the only implemented adapter. Other configured providers are reported as not implemented. No response is substituted.
- Incoming captures are gitignored until a person copies an unmodified file into `records/public/`.
- No silent rewriting of original responses.
